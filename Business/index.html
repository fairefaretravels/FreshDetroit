The Problem

Good food can have a short window.

  • → Local growers produce genuinely valuable food.
  • → Harvest timing creates urgency — produce doesn't wait.
  • → Consumers often don't know what's available nearby, right now.
  • → Small growers may not have sophisticated digital sales tools.
  • → Usable food can become waste when supply and demand aren't efficiently connected.

06 / 12
Why Detroit

Detroit is our first market.

Detroit provides the initial environment for testing the platform with local growers, gardens, consumers, and food businesses — an established urban agriculture and community gardening ecosystem, and the market this grant opportunity is built around.

The objective is a repeatable Detroit pilot model — proven with real growers and real transactions — that can inform expansion into additional cities later. No other markets are claimed or committed to at this stage.
